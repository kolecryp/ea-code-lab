ABOUT THE LAB

Learn by building. Improve by testing.

EA Code Lab is a practical learning and development resource for people working with MQL5 and automated trading systems.

What we believe

Good trading software begins with clear requirements, readable code, careful testing, and honest reporting of limitations. A backtest is evidence to evaluate—not a promise about what happens next.

What you'll find here

We publish programming walkthroughs, debugging methods, system-design notes, and practical resources for developers at different stages. We aim to explain both what a solution does and why it works.

Our standard

We strive to distinguish tested examples from illustrative snippets, correct mistakes transparently, and avoid claims of guaranteed trading returns.
